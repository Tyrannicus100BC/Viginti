Bring the three pattern upgrades closer together

Pair upgrades currently lag Straight and Flush. Increase Pair's regular cash ceiling and multiplier range; reduce Straight and Flush cash ranges, and trim their stronger benefits.

Recommendation only. Production payouts, reward generation and saved cards are unchanged. The simulation trigger counter was corrected to count upgrade applications, including grouped Pairs and the extra applications on longer Straights and Flushes.

Scope and verification

  • Both play heuristics use public observations; neither is an optimal-play solver. No external reference data was required.
  • The final range comparisons cover 40,000 rounds and 7200 exact production settlement checks. Reported ± values are 95% sampling margins across round-averaged roll effects; they do not include uncertainty in bot quality or describe random-roll variance.
  • The range audit holds final hands fixed. The stack audit changes play in response to actual rolled upgrades. Stack changes are paired by trial seed; extra draws can change subsequent dealer outcomes.
  • These tests isolate Raise effects. Composite Reward cards also contain deck/relic changes. The older natural-run selector currently skips composite Reward IDs, so its survival results were excluded.
  • Tradeoff drawbacks and Win/Viginti/Lose/Bust rolls were not calibrated here. Equal gross positive effects need not yield equal net Tradeoff value.
  • If implemented, introduce a new Raise ID version and preserve v10 decoding ranges. Replacing validation ranges in place can invalidate previously saved rolls.
  • 81 focused tests passed. The changed simulation code and audit script passed lint; the script passed syntax and small-run checks. Whole-project type-check reported 162 errors in other files; none were reported in the changed simulation files.
